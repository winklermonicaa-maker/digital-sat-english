Expression of Ideas — Transition Questions

How to Answer Transition Questions

These questions ask you to choose the transition word or phrase that best connects two sentences. Use the two-step process below, then match the relationship to one of the four patterns.

Two-Step Process

  • 1Ask: How is sentence 2 related to sentence 1? Read both sentences carefully before looking at the choices.
  • 2Identify the relationship — Direction (continue or contrast) · Information (example / clarify / emphasize) · Logic (cause or comparison) · Time (sequence)
Pattern 1

Direction of Ideas

Continues Same Direction

Both sentences point the same way — like two bullet points in the same list. Ask: could I write "Reason 1 / Reason 2" before each? If yes → continues.

furthermore moreover in addition

The museum expanded its hours to attract more visitors.

Furthermore, it reduced ticket prices on weekdays.

Contrast Change Direction

Sentence 2 weakens, limits, or qualifies sentence 1. If it surprises you → contrast.

however nevertheless although on the other hand instead

The treatment showed promising results in early trials.

However, later studies failed to replicate the findings.

Concession Admit Then Shift

The author briefly admits a point before shifting away from it. The concession doesn't cancel the main argument — it just acknowledges it.

granted admittedly of course

The hike is challenging for beginners.

Granted, most visitors still complete it without difficulty.

Pattern 2

Adding Information

Example General → Specific

Sentence 1 makes a general claim; sentence 2 provides a specific instance of it.

for example for instance specifically to demonstrate

Many animals adapt to extreme environments.

For example, Arctic foxes grow thicker fur during winter.

Strengthen / Emphasize More Extreme

Sentence 2 does NOT add a new idea — it makes sentence 1 more extreme or precise.

in fact indeed actually even

The novel challenges traditional ideas about heroism.

In fact, its protagonist refuses to solve the conflict at all.

Clarify Restate Simpler

Sentence 2 says the same idea again in clearer or simpler terms.

in other words that is

The desert receives minimal precipitation each year.

In other words, rainfall there is extremely rare.

Pattern 3

Logical Relationships

Cause & Effect Result

Sentence 2 shows what happens because of sentence 1.

therefore thus so consequently

The road was covered in ice overnight.

Therefore, schools were closed the next morning.

Comparison Relative Evaluation

Sentence 2 evaluates one thing relative to another — showing similarity or difference, but without disagreement or surprise.

by comparison similarly likewise in contrast

The 2001 solar cycle produced about 180 sunspots.

By comparison, the 2008 cycle produced only two.

Pattern 4

Time Relationships

Sequence of Events Time Passes

Only use time transitions when real time passes or you notice steps in a process. Don't confuse sequence with cause-and-effect.

then later finally subsequently ultimately previously prior to before

The researchers collected the samples from the field.

Then, they analyzed them in the laboratory.

Question 1

Marine biologist Sylvia Earle has spent decades advocating for the protection of ocean ecosystems. she has led more than a hundred deep-sea expeditions and set multiple records for solo diving depth.

Which choice completes the text with the most logical transition?

C – Indeed is correct. The second sentence doesn't introduce a new idea — it strengthens and supports the first by giving concrete evidence of Earle's commitment (expeditions, diving records). This is a Pattern 2 strengthen/emphasize relationship. "However" and "In contrast" signal contradiction that isn't present; "Therefore" would imply her expeditions were caused by her advocacy, which distorts the logic.
Question 2

The city of Pompeii was buried under several meters of volcanic ash when Mount Vesuvius erupted in 79 CE. the disaster preserved the city so thoroughly that archaeologists have been able to reconstruct daily Roman life in remarkable detail.

Which choice completes the text with the most logical transition?

D – Nevertheless is correct. The eruption destroyed Pompeii, yet that same event preserved it for archaeologists — an outcome that contradicts what you'd expect from the first sentence. "Nevertheless" signals that the second sentence holds true in spite of the first, which captures that surprise. "Similarly" and "Subsequently" don't signal contrast; "Therefore" would make the preservation a straightforward result of the eruption, missing the unexpected nature of the outcome.
Question 3

Researchers studying ancient trade routes have long focused on the Silk Road connecting China to the Mediterranean. recent archaeological finds suggest that equally significant maritime networks linked East Africa, the Arabian Peninsula, and South Asia centuries earlier than previously thought.

Which choice completes the text with the most logical transition?

C – However is correct. The first sentence describes what researchers have focused on; the second challenges that focus by pointing to a different, overlooked network. Sentence 2 redirects and limits the first — a classic contrast (Pattern 1). "Therefore" implies a result; "For instance" would make sentence 2 a specific example of Silk Road research; "Moreover" would add to the first claim rather than push back on it.
Question 4

Composer Hildegard von Bingen wrote some of the most complex sacred music of the twelfth century, including elaborate chants for her convent's liturgies. she produced theological texts, poetry, and detailed writings on natural medicine — an output remarkable for any scholar of the era, let alone a cloistered nun.

Which choice completes the text with the most logical transition?

C – Moreover is correct. The second sentence adds more evidence of von Bingen's extraordinary output — theology, poetry, medicine — on top of the music already mentioned. This is a "continues / same direction" relationship (Pattern 1). "However" would introduce a contrast that isn't there; "Therefore" implies her writing was caused by her music; "For instance" would mean the second sentence is a specific example of the first, which it isn't.
Question 5

Early astronomers believed that the Sun revolved around the Earth, a model known as the geocentric view. this perspective seemed to align perfectly with everyday observation — after all, the Sun appears to rise and set each day.

Which choice completes the text with the most logical transition?

D – Admittedly is correct. The passage acknowledges that the geocentric view had a reasonable basis — it matched what people could see. This is a concession (Pattern 1): the author briefly admits a point (the model seemed logical) before the larger argument moves on. "Nevertheless" would signal a contrast coming after; "Subsequently" implies time passing; "By comparison" requires a second thing being measured against something else.
Question 6

Engineers first designed the prototype in a small workshop with minimal equipment. the team transferred the project to a full-scale facility, where they ran extensive stress tests over the following months.

Which choice completes the text with the most logical transition?

B – Subsequently is correct. Two events happen in clear chronological order: first the prototype stage, then the move to a full-scale facility. Real time passes between steps, and the passage describes a process — this is a time / sequence relationship (Pattern 4). "In other words" would mean the second sentence restates the first; "For example" would make the second a specific instance of the first; "Likewise" signals similarity, not sequence.
